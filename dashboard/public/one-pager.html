The Agent

A four-agent swarm to be the best version of yourself.

Body, mind, nutrition, sports. Holy is your holistic health & longevity coaching squad: four Claude-powered coaches, each trained on its own discipline and on your own data. A normal coach hands you a plan once — Holy recalculates yours continuously, reviewing the estimated plan against actuals and your health KPIs, and adapting it overnight.

What

A lead Coach for training & load, Nutri for fuelling, Doc for sports medicine, Mind for sports psychology. Each persona is trained on its own specialist playbook — and coaches from your data, not generic advice.

Impact

Four experts almost nobody has, without four experts' noise. A dropped HRV becomes a decision, not a 11pm search.

2 The Daily Brief

Adapts overnight to your own data

Impact

This is the gold. A normal coach writes the plan once; Holy reviews estimated plan against actuals and recalibrates, so the plan bends to reality instead of scoring you against it.

Daily pipeline

Sync → route → fan out → one brief

The trigger tables are the product: a specialist that fires every day is expensive wallpaper, and one that never fires is a broken tripwire.

HHOLY
01

SyncTrigger

Wearable and manual quick-log data pulled into one store. Local files authoritative; dashboard downstream.

02

Read the teamAgentic

Vault notes from sibling agents, discovered by listing the directory. A heavy work week changes how this week gets paced.

03

RouteTrigger

Trigger tables decide who fires. Nutri always. Doc on N-day threshold breaches or symptoms. Mind on phase, failures, flat language.

04

Fan outAgentic

Fired specialists run in parallel, never serially. Each returns a short, attributable read in its own voice.

05

SynthesiseAgentic

Coach recalibrates the plan against your actuals, then ships one brief: the read, every specialist quoted, three micro-actions, one closer. ≤700 characters.

Model routing Sync, parsing & threshold checks → Haiku. Specialist reasoning & the brief → Sonnet. The conditional triggers do the cost control: specialists run only when their tables fire.
