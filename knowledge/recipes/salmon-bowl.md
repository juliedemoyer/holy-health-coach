## Ingredients
- 2 × 150 g salmon fillets, skin on
- 1 tbsp white miso paste
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 200 g brown rice (dry)
- 200 g shelled edamame (frozen is fine)
- 1 ripe avocado, sliced
- 1 cucumber, ribboned
- 2 spring onions, sliced
- furikake or sesame seeds, lime wedges to serve

## Steps
1. Cook brown rice per packet. While it cooks, whisk miso, soy,
   honey, and sesame oil. Smear over salmon flesh.
2. Bake salmon at 200 °C for 12 min until just done. (Air fryer: 10 min.)
3. Boil edamame 3 min, drain.
4. Build bowls: rice base, salmon on top, edamame, avocado, cucumber,
   spring onion. Furikake + lime to finish.

## Why this one (Nutri)

The recovery gold standard. Salmon delivers ~30 g protein + omega-3s
(reduces post-session inflammation). Brown rice replenishes glycogen,
edamame adds another 17 g protein per cup. **Eat within 90 min of a
hard session for best glycogen replenishment.** Doubles cleanly for
batch-cooking; rice + edamame keep 3 days, salmon eats best fresh.
