## Ingredients
- 250 g dry green or puy lentils (or 2 cans, drained)
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 1 tin (400 g) chopped tomatoes
- 600 ml vegetable stock
- 200 g baby spinach
- 100 g feta, crumbled
- 1 lemon (zest + juice)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, salt + pepper

## Steps
1. Sweat onion, carrot, and garlic in olive oil 5 min. Add cumin and
   paprika, stir 30 sec.
2. Add lentils, tomatoes, and stock. Simmer 25 min until lentils are
   tender (less for canned — 10 min).
3. Stir in spinach until wilted. Off heat, add lemon zest + juice.
4. Serve with feta on top, optional dollop of Greek yogurt.

## Why this one (Nutri)

The iron play. Lentils + spinach is the textbook plant-iron pairing,
and the **tomatoes + lemon juice provide the vitamin C** that boosts
non-heme iron absorption ~2×. 24 g protein per serve mostly from
lentils. Batches beautifully — eat for 3 days, freezes for 2 months.
Pair with brown rice or sourdough toast on long-run days for more carbs.
