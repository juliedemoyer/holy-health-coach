## Ingredients
- 80 g rolled oats
- 250 ml almond milk (or water; **avoid dairy if your gut is sensitive**)
- 1 ripe banana, sliced
- 2 tbsp honey or maple syrup
- pinch of salt
- 250 ml black coffee or green tea (timing per below)

## Steps
1. Cook oats with milk and salt 4 min. Top with banana and honey.
2. Drink coffee at the same time you eat (caffeine takes ~45 min to
   peak; for a 9:15 start, eat at 6:30 with coffee).

## Why this one (Nutri)

Race-day breakfast. **3–4 hours before start.** 95 g carbs gets you
into the 1.5 g/kg pre-race carbohydrate load zone. Low fibre,
low fat, low protein — your gut will thank you at km 30.

**Test this in your race-rehearsal long run at week 10**, not on race
morning. If you can't do dairy near a race, use water instead of
almond milk; the carb count holds.

**Caffeine timing matters more than dose.** 3 mg/kg (≈180 mg for a
scale to body mass) 45–60 min before start. Don't introduce caffeine on
race day if you don't drink it normally.
