## Ingredients
- 6 tbsp chia seeds
- 600 ml unsweetened almond milk (or whole milk for higher protein)
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 banana, sliced
- 30 g walnuts, roughly chopped
- pinch of cinnamon

## Steps
1. Whisk chia, milk, maple, vanilla, and cinnamon in a jar. Refrigerate
   8 hours or overnight (give it a stir at the 30-min mark to avoid
   clumping).
2. Top each serve with banana + walnuts when ready to eat.

## Why this one (Nutri)

The make-Sunday-eat-all-week play. Chia delivers omega-3 ALA + 5 g
fibre per tbsp; walnuts pile on more omega-3 + magnesium (cramp
protection). Sits in the fridge 4 days. **Pair with Greek yogurt** to
lift the protein from 14 → 26 g per serve on training days. Best
post-easy-run snack on busy weekday afternoons.
