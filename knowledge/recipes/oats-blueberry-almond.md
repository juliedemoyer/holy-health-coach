## Ingredients
- 60 g rolled oats
- 200 ml unsweetened almond milk (or whole milk)
- 150 g full-fat Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp almond butter
- 100 g blueberries (fresh or frozen)
- 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
- pinch of cinnamon, pinch of salt

## Steps
1. Combine oats, milk, salt, and cinnamon in a small saucepan.
   Simmer 4–5 min until creamy. (Microwave: 90 sec, stir, 60 sec.)
2. Top with Greek yogurt, blueberries, almond butter, and a drizzle of
   honey.

## Why this one (Nutri)

The default training-day breakfast. 32 g protein gets you to the
1.6 g/kg floor in one meal — almost. Slow-release carbs from oats,
fast-release from honey + berries (anthocyanins help recovery, modestly).
Greek yogurt is the cheapest 20 g protein you'll find. **Swap blueberries
for raspberries in iron-focus weeks** (more vitamin C boosts iron uptake
from anything else on the plate).
