## Ingredients
- 200 g spaghetti or penne (or 250 g for a heavier pre-long-run load)
- 1 tin (400 g) chopped tomatoes
- 2 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1 small onion, diced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 50 g parmesan, grated
- handful basil leaves
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- pinch of sugar, salt + pepper

## Steps
1. Boil pasta to al dente. Reserve 100 ml pasta water before draining.
2. While it cooks: sweat onion + garlic in olive oil 4 min. Add
   tomatoes, tomato paste, sugar, salt. Simmer 12 min.
3. Combine pasta with sauce + a splash of pasta water. Off heat, stir
   in parmesan and torn basil.

## Why this one (Nutri)

Boring on purpose. The night before a long run is **not** the time to
try a new dish. 110 g carbs gets you to 1.8 g/kg the night before —
right in the pre-race carb-load zone. Low fibre, low fat, low risk of
gut surprises tomorrow. **Eat 12+ hours before the run, not 4** —
overnight digestion is the goal.

Skip if you're doing this Saturday-evening for a Sunday morning long
run; eat by 19:00 latest.
