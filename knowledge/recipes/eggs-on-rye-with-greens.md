## Ingredients
- 2 eggs
- 2 slices rye sourdough
- 1 handful baby spinach or chard
- ½ avocado, sliced
- 1 tsp butter or olive oil
- 1 garlic clove, sliced thinly
- chilli flakes, sea salt, lemon

## Steps
1. Toast rye. Sauté garlic in butter 30 sec, add greens, wilt 1 min.
   Squeeze lemon, salt.
2. In the same pan, fry the eggs to your liking (runny yolks pair with
   the toast).
3. Stack: rye, greens, avocado, eggs, chilli flakes.

## Why this one (Nutri)

The 10-minute weekday default when oats fatigue sets in. Eggs are the
cheapest 12 g protein each, the fat keeps blood sugar steady through
mid-morning meetings. Rye sourdough beats white toast for sustained
energy. **Add a squeeze of lemon to the spinach** — same iron-absorption
trick as the lentil stew.
