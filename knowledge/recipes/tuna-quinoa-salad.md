## Ingredients
- 100 g quinoa (dry)
- 1 can (140 g drained) tuna in olive oil or spring water
- 1 can (240 g drained) cannellini or butter beans
- 1 red pepper, diced
- ½ cucumber, diced
- 50 g rocket or baby spinach
- 30 g feta, crumbled (optional)
- juice of 1 lemon, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp Dijon, salt + pepper

## Steps
1. Cook quinoa in salted water 12–15 min until fluffy. Cool slightly.
2. Whisk dressing: lemon juice, olive oil, Dijon, salt, pepper.
3. Toss quinoa, tuna (drained, flaked), beans, pepper, cucumber,
   greens, and dressing. Top with feta if using.

## Why this one (Nutri)

The desk-lunch champion. 38 g protein from a tin and a can — no
cooking complexity, no mid-day cooking smell, eats well at room
temperature. **Use sustainable tuna** (pole-and-line; the labels say
so). Doubles as a Tuesday-Wednesday batch. If iron is the focus that
week, swap tuna for sardines (heavier on heme iron) and add lemon zest.
