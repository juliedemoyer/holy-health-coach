# Coach's playbook

> This file is Coach's source of truth for the marathon-specific
> micro-actions Coach ships every morning. Concise rules, not essays.
> Nutri, Doc, and Mind read this too — it sets the shared assumptions
> for the build.

Race: **whatever is in `config/race.json`**
20-week build · session goal and athlete details in `config/`
Tune-ups: 10 km mid-July · half-marathon mid-August

## Phase rules

### Base (>16 weeks out)

1. **Volume builds 5–10% per week** until you hit 90% of peak weekly km.
2. **80/20 rule** — 80% of running at conversational pace, 20% at
   tempo/threshold/intervals. Don't invert it.
3. **One long run per week** — start at 14 km, build by 1–2 km each
   week toward 32–35 km in peak.
4. **Two strength sessions per week, non-negotiable.** Compound lifts
   (squat, deadlift, single-leg). Skip a run before you skip strength.
5. **One easy day after every hard day.** Easy means ≤70% max HR.
6. **Aerobic base over speed.** Speed is the topping; the cake is
   easy mileage.

### Build (16–8 weeks out)

7. **Add one workout per week** — tempo, fartlek, or hill repeats.
   Quality > quantity; cancel the workout if recovery is bad.
8. **Marathon-pace work appears** — finish long runs with 4–8 km at
   marathon pace from week 12 onward.
9. **Practice race fuel on every long run** — 60g carbs/hour. Try gel
   brands, sports drinks, real food. Find what doesn't upset the gut.
10. **One race-rehearsal long run** at week 10: full kit, race-morning
    breakfast, race-pace fuel cadence. Anything that breaks here gets
    swapped before peak.

### Peak (8–4 weeks out)

11. **Highest weekly volume** in this window — typically 60–70 km/week
    for sub-3:30 finishers.
12. **Longest long run** at week 6–7 (32–35 km). Don't go over 35.
13. **Race-pace tempo runs** weekly — 12–18 km at marathon pace.
14. **Full taper visualisation rehearsal** at week 5 — pretend it's race
    week, mentally rehearse the morning + first 10 km.

### Taper (4–1 weeks out)

15. **Volume drops by 30/40/60%** in weeks 3/2/1. Don't taper your
    intensity; keep the workouts spicy at lower volume.
16. **Strength: drop the heavy compound work in week 2.** Maintenance
    movements only.
17. **Sleep is the big lever.** Aim for +30 min vs base. The "extra
    sleep two weeks before" study (Mah et al.) holds.
18. **Carb load weeks 1**, not 3. 8–10g carbs/kg in the final 36–48h.
19. **Predict and pre-empt the taper-tantrum** — restlessness, doubt,
    phantom niggles are the ride. Mind owns this.
20. **No new shoes, no new fuel, no new kit** in this window. Race in
    what you trained in.

### Race week

21. **Sleep target hits 2–3 days before**, not the night before. Race-
    night-1 sleep doesn't predict performance; race-night-2 does.
22. **Hydrate in pulses**, not in one chug. Sip throughout the day.
23. **Race day breakfast: 3–4 hours pre-start.** 1.5g carbs/kg, low
    fibre, low fat. Test in the rehearsal long run.
24. **If fever or vomiting in the 48h before — do not race.** Doc has
    veto power here.
25. **Pacing rule of three:** km 1–10 feels too easy, km 11–30 feels
    right, km 31–42.2 feels hard. If km 1 feels right, you're going
    too fast.

### Race day

26. **Negative split or even split** beats positive split for PB
    attempts. Go out at goal pace +5 sec/km for the first 5 km.
27. **Fuel every 4–5 km from km 8 onward.** 60g carbs/hour minimum.
28. **The Brandenburg Gate at km 41.6 is the moment.** Everything
    before it is just getting there. Mind has the mantra.
29. **Walk the aid stations** if you need to drink properly. 5 sec
    saves you from spilling 500 ml.
30. **Smile at km 30.** It tricks the brain, lowers perceived effort.
    Sounds dumb; it works.

### Post-race

31. **24-hour rule:** no analysis, no recovery walk, no "what next."
    Eat, sleep, celebrate or grieve.
32. **One week of zero structured running.** Walk, swim, easy spin.
    Body needs it; brain needs it.
33. **Two weeks of easy running** before any structured work returns.
34. **Post-race blues are predictable** — even after a PB. Mind owns
    this; book a deload, plan the next thing in 4–6 weeks not 4–6 days.

## Cross-cutting rules

### Pescatarian fuelling (Nutri co-owns)

35. **Protein: 1.6–2.0 g/kg body weight.** Scale it to the body mass in `config/athlete.json`
    daily, more on long-run days.
36. **Iron + B12 are the perpetual concerns.** Sardines, mussels,
    eggs, lentils + vitamin C, fortified cereal. Ferritin checked
    yearly minimum.
37. **Carbs are not the enemy during build.** Adjust quantity to load.
38. **Don't restrict during build.** Body comp shifts come from
    training + sleep + protein + time, not from a deficit.

### Sleep

39. **Target 7.5–8.5 hours.** Below 6.5h for 3+ days = no hard session.
40. **Wind-down ritual**: screens off 30 min before bed, dim lights,
    cool room (18°C). Mind owns the headspace; physiology is shared.

### Cycle awareness (V3, opt-in: ON)

41. **Luteal phase:** HRV runs 5–10 ms below baseline, RHR 1–3 bpm
    above. Doc normalises before flagging anomalies.
42. **Late luteal / menstrual:** higher iron in meals, one extra
    recovery slot in the training week.
43. **Follicular:** lean into intensity. Body absorbs hard sessions
    better.

### Cross-agent (sibling vault notes)

44. **Sibling agent flags a high-stakes week** (interview, deadline,
    travel) → bias toward sleep, reduce intensity, no new workouts.
45. **Sibling flags a heavy week post-hoc** → schedule a deload sooner
    than planned.
46. **High-recovery + nothing flagged** → green light for the heaviest
    work in the plan.

### Tune-up race rules

47. **Use them as fitness tests, not PB attempts.** 10 km at 5K-PB
    pace + 10 sec/km, not all-out.
48. **Practice race fuelling on the half-marathon test.** Gels every
    5 km from km 5 onward. The half is a dress rehearsal.
49. **Don't taper hard for either** — they're inside the build, not
    above it. 1-day shake-out the day before; race; back to normal.
50. **If a tune-up goes badly**, Mind handles the reframe; Coach
    re-runs the plan diagnostics; Nutri checks fuel; Doc checks
    physiology. All four meet, in order. Fix one variable, not all
    four.
